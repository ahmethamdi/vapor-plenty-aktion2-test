# VaporAktion2 – promotion "buy 6 ELFLIQ, get 1 gift free"

From N promotion items (default 6) in the cart, the customer can choose a free gift (ELFX Pod Kit or 1 ELFLIQ) in a box on the cart page. The gift is added at its normal price and a fixed-amount promotion coupon configured in the back end makes it free. Max. 1 gift per order; not combinable with other coupons. See the German user guide for settings and container links.
